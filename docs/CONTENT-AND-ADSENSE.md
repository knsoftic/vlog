# PineCast TV — Content, Trending Topics, AI Images aur AdSense Guide

Yeh guide teen sawalon ka jawab hai:

1. Site ke liye trending topics kaise dhoondein
2. Posts ke liye AI se images kaise banayein (aur kya nahi karna)
3. AdSense apply karne ke liye kya kya criteria poore karne hain

> **Sabse pehle yeh:** aapki site par abhi jo 5 sample vlogs seed hue hain, woh **AdSense apply karne se pehle delete karne hain**. Sample/placeholder content "site under construction" category mein aata hai aur yehi AdSense rejection ki sabse aam wajah hai.

---

## Hissa 1 — Niche aur Trending Topics

### 1.1 Pehle niche decide karein (yeh sabse bara faisla hai)

AdSense ki kamai traffic se nahi, **advertiser demand** se hoti hai. Ek hi topic par 20 guna farq par jata hai.

Third-party publishers ke reported RPM ranges (yeh Google ka official data nahi hai, sirf indicative hai — asal kamai traffic quality, country aur season par depend karti hai):

| Niche | Reported RPM range (US traffic) | Vlog format ke liye kaisa? |
|---|---|---|
| Personal finance, insurance, legal | $40 – $110 | Mushkil — expertise chahiye, YMYL rules sakht |
| Software / SaaS / tech reviews | $10 – $35 | Bahut acha — screen recording se vlogs ban jate hain |
| Home improvement, DIY, home decor | $8 – $25 | Bahut acha — visual content, abhi trending bhi hai |
| Automotive, tools, gear reviews | $8 – $20 | Acha — product demo vlogs |
| Travel (US domestic) | $5 – $15 | Acha lekin competitive |
| Entertainment, celebrity, news | $2 – $5 | Sabse kam — is se bachein |

**PineCast TV ke liye meri recommendation:** ek **primary niche** chunein aur 80% content usi par banayein.

Sabse behtar combination aapke liye:

> **Home & DIY + Practical Tech** — "ghar ko behtar banane" wale projects, tools, aur woh gadgets/apps jo rozmarra kaam asaan karte hain.

Wajah:
- Video format ke liye natural hai (before/after, step-by-step)
- Google Trends par abhi bohat strong demand hai (neeche dekhein)
- RPM `$8–$25` — entertainment se 4 guna behtar
- YMYL (finance/health) ki tarah sakht E-E-A-T requirements nahi
- AI images legitimately use ho sakti hain (concept, illustration, thumbnail)

### 1.2 Abhi ke actual trending topics (September 2026)

Google Trends ke Summergeist 2026 report aur trending data se yeh confirm shuda rising searches hain:

**Home decor / DIY (yeh aapka main target hai)**

| Topic | Trend |
|---|---|
| Moody & maximalist home decor | US mein trending |
| Harlequin wallpaper | Breakout (past month) |
| "neon beige color" | +400% (2026) |
| "warm greige paint colors" | Double se zyada |
| Cottagecore aesthetic | Breakout |
| Coastal chic interior design | Breakout |
| "Vintage" (overall) | All-time high 2026 |
| Vintage braided rugs, wood coffee tables | Top trending vintage items |
| "how to polish wood furniture" | +450% YTD |
| "things to bedazzle ideas" | +350% |
| "how to bedazzle something" | +200% |

**Seasonal (abhi likhne ka best waqt)**

- 2026–27 winter weather predictions → winter home prep, heating cost, insulation
- Fall/winter home decor transitions

Yeh topics **evergreen bhi hain aur trending bhi** — AdSense ke liye ideal combination.

**Kis se bachein:** celebrity news, movie showtimes, sports scores, elections. Yeh abhi #1 trending hain lekin RPM ~$2–4 hai, 48 ghante mein mar jate hain, aur AdSense review mein "low value" lag sakte hain.

### 1.3 Topics dhoondne ka permanent system (roz 15 minute)

Sirf aaj ke trends kaafi nahi — yeh 5 sources har hafte check karein:

1. **Google Trends → Trending Now** (trends.google.com/trending) — country USA set karein, category filter lagayein. "Past 4 hours" ki jagah **"Past 7 days"** dekhein, us se stable topics milte hain.
2. **Google Trends → Explore** — apna keyword daalein, "United States", "Past 12 months", aur neeche **"Related queries → Rising"** dekhein. Yehi asli sona hai.
3. **Aapki apni site ka Search Console** — Admin → SEO → Search Console. Wahan **"Top queries"** mein woh keywords dikhte hain jin par aap already impressions le rahe hain lekin position 8–20 par hain. Un par behtar post likhein, jaldi rank hoti hai.
4. **Aapki site ka internal search log** — Admin → Analytics → Site Search. Wahan **"Zero-result searches"** section hai. Log aapki site par kya dhoondh rahe hain jo mila nahi — yeh direct content ideas hain aur is ka koi muqabla nahi kyunke sirf aapke paas hai.
5. **YouTube search suggest** — YouTube par apna keyword type karein aur suggestions dekhein. Yeh actual video demand dikhate hain, jo vlog site ke liye Google se zyada relevant hai.

Ek chhota rule: agar koi topic Google Trends par 6 mahine se consistent hai **aur** aaj rise kar raha hai, wohi likhein.

### 1.4 Pehle 30 posts ka plan

AdSense apply karne se pehle **kam az kam 25–30 solid posts** honi chahiye. Yeh distribution rakhein:

| Type | Kitne | Misal |
|---|---|---|
| How-to / step-by-step | 12 | "How to Polish Old Wood Furniture Without Stripping It" |
| Buying guide / comparison | 6 | "Best Budget Paint Sprayers for Small Rooms in 2026" |
| Trend explainer | 5 | "Warm Greige: Why Everyone Is Repainting in 2026" |
| Before/after project | 4 | "I Turned a $40 Thrift Dresser Into a Coastal Piece" |
| Mistakes / warnings | 3 | "7 Cottagecore Decor Mistakes That Make a Room Look Cheap" |

Har post ka target: **800–1500 words** original text + 1 video (agar hai) + 3–5 images.

## Hissa 2 — AI se Images banana

### 2.1 Yeh baat sabse pehle samajh lein

Google AI images ko **mana nahi karta**. Google ki policy content ke **maqsad** par hai, tareeqe par nahi:

> Scaled content abuse = AI se bohat saare pages banana jin mein user ke liye koi value na ho, sirf ranking ke liye.
> Agar content useful, original aur helpful hai, to AI ka istemal policy ke khilaf nahi.

Matlab:
- **Theek hai:** AI se featured image, thumbnail, illustration, diagram, background banana
- **Theek nahi:** AI se 200 pages ek din mein banana, ya AI images ko "asli photo" bata kar pesh karna

### 2.2 Kaunsa AI tool use karein

| Tool | Commercial use | Free tier | Kis kaam ke liye behtar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adobe Firefly** | Haan, licensed data par trained + IP indemnification | Mehdood credits | Sabse safe option, client/commercial kaam |
| **Google Gemini** | Haan | Achi free allowance | Roz ka blog image, sabse asaan |
| **Ideogram** | Haan (free tier bhi, attribution ke sath) | 25/din | **Text wali images** — thumbnails jin par likhai ho |
| **Microsoft Copilot / Bing Image Creator** | Haan | Free | Quick concept images |
| **Leonardo AI** | Haan | 150 credits/din | Consistent style series |
| **Midjourney** | Haan (paid) | Nahi | Best quality, magazine-level |
| **Flux.1 / Stable Diffusion** | Haan (self-hosted) | Unlimited (apna server) | Bulk, poora control |

**Aapke liye:** roz ke kaam ke liye **Gemini** ya **Ideogram** (thumbnails ke liye Ideogram behtar hai kyunke us par text sahi aata hai), aur zyada important posts ke liye **Firefly**.

> Har tool ki terms waqtan waqtan badalti hain. Jo tool chunein, us ke "Commercial use" section ko ek baar khud parh lein aur screenshot rakh lein.

### 2.3 Practical workflow (har post ke liye)

**1. Featured image (1200×675, 16:9)**

Prompt ka formula:

```
[subject], [setting], [style], [lighting], [camera], no text, 16:9
```

Misal:

```
A restored vintage walnut coffee table in a sunlit living room,
warm greige walls, cottagecore styling with dried flowers,
soft natural window light, shot on 35mm film, shallow depth of field,
photorealistic, no text, no watermark, 16:9
```

**2. In-article images (800px wide)**

Step-by-step posts mein har major step par ek image. Prompt mein `close-up`, `overhead view`, `hands working on` jaise words se variety layein.

**3. Thumbnail (Ideogram, text ke sath)**

```
Bold YouTube-style thumbnail, split before-and-after of an old dresser
and a restored coastal blue dresser, large white text "40 DOLLAR FLIP",
high contrast, clean composition
```

**4. Upload aur optimize**

Aapke CMS mein yeh automatic ho jata hai — upload karte hi WebP variants (thumb 400px, medium 800px, large 1400px) ban jate hain. Aapko sirf yeh karna hai:

- Admin → Media Library → Upload
- **Alt text zaroor likhein** — yeh SEO aur accessibility dono ke liye lazmi hai, aur post ki Quality Checklist mein bhi check hota hai
- Alt text descriptive ho: "Restored walnut coffee table with warm greige wall behind it" — na ke "image1" ya keyword stuffing

### 2.4 AI images ke sath yeh kabhi na karein

| Na karein | Wajah |
|---|---|
| Asli logon ki AI photo banana (celebrity, politician) | Publisher policy violation + legal risk |
| AI image ko "asli project photo" bata kar pesh karna | Misleading content — policy violation |
| Brand logos, product packaging AI se banana | Trademark issue |
| "Before/After" AI se banana jo aapne khud nahi kiya | Deceptive claim — AdSense reject kar sakta hai |
| Sirf AI images se page bharna, text kam | "Mostly images/videos" wale sites approve nahi hote |
| Medical, legal, financial diagrams AI se banana | Ghalat information ka risk (YMYL) |

**Sabse zaroori:** agar post "maine yeh dresser restore kiya" keh rahi hai, to **asli photos** honi chahiyein. AI images tab use karein jab woh **illustration ya concept** hon, dawa nahi.

Behtareen tareeqa: **asli photos + AI se banaye gaye diagrams/illustrations/thumbnails**. Yeh combination sabse strong hai.

## Hissa 3 — AdSense Criteria (official requirements)

### 3.1 Bunyadi eligibility (Google ki official shara'it)

| Requirement | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| Umar | Kam az kam **18 saal**. Kam umar ho to walid/guardian ke naam par apply karein. |
| Site ka control | Site ke **HTML source code** tak rasai honi chahiye (verification code `<head>` mein lagana hota hai). Aapke CMS mein yeh Settings se ho jata hai. |
| Content | **High-quality, original**, aur audience ko attract karne wala. |
| Policy | AdSense Program Policies aur Google Publisher Policies ki paabandi. |
| Account | Ek publisher ka **sirf ek** AdSense account. |

### 3.2 Rejection ki official wajuhat (aur inka hal)

Google in wajuhat se account reject karta hai:

| Rejection reason | Aapko kya karna hai |
|---|---|
| **Insufficient content** — "too little text", site "under construction" | 25–30 posts, har ek 800+ words. Sample content delete karein. |
| **Mostly images/videos** — "sites that contain mostly images, videos or Flash animations may not be approved" | **Yeh vlog site ke liye sabse bara khatra hai.** Har vlog ke sath 800+ words ka asal article likhein — sirf embed + 2 lines kaafi nahi. |
| **Low value content** — thin affiliate, auto-generated pages | Har post mein apni asli raye, tajurba, ya original testing daalein. |
| **Navigation issues** — broken links, pop-ups, unlaunched pages | Admin → SEO → Broken Links check karein. Koi "Coming Soon" page na ho. |
| **Policy violations** | Admin → Monetization → Policy Checklist chalayein. |
| **Questionable traffic** — paid-to-click, spam email traffic | Kabhi traffic kharidein nahi. Organic aur social hi rakhein. |
| **Unsupported language** | Content English mein rakhein (US audience target hai). |
| **Duplicate account** | Purana AdSense account hai to naya na banayein. |

### 3.3 Apply karne se pehle checklist

Aapke CMS mein yeh **Admin → Monetization → Policy Checklist** par automatic check hoti hai. Manually bhi confirm karein:

**Content**
- [ ] 25–30 published posts, har ek 800+ words original
- [ ] Saare sample/seed posts delete
- [ ] Koi copy-paste ya spun content nahi
- [ ] Har post mein featured image + alt text
- [ ] Har vlog post mein video ke sath asal likha hua article

**Zaroori pages (site par live aur footer mein linked)**
- [ ] Privacy Policy — is mein Google, cookies, aur third-party advertising ka zikr hona chahiye
- [ ] Cookie Policy
- [ ] Terms & Conditions
- [ ] Disclaimer
- [ ] About Us — asli maloomat, aap kaun hain, site kis liye hai
- [ ] Contact Us — working form ya email

> Yeh saare pages aapke CMS mein pehle se seed hain, lekin un ka **default text apne mutabiq edit karna zaroori hai**. Template text jaisa ka waisa chhodna "low value" signal deta hai.

**Technical**
- [ ] Domain live hai aur HTTPS par chal raha hai
- [ ] `https://pinecasttv.com/sitemap.xml` khul raha hai
- [ ] `https://pinecasttv.com/robots.txt` khul raha hai
- [ ] `https://pinecasttv.com/ads.txt` khul raha hai (publisher ID ke sath)
- [ ] Google Search Console verified aur sitemap submit
- [ ] Mobile par site theek chal rahi hai
- [ ] Koi broken link nahi
- [ ] Site kam az kam **2–4 hafte purani** ho (naya domain foran approve nahi hota)

**Settings (aapke admin panel mein)**
- [ ] Cookie consent enabled + Consent Mode v2 on
- [ ] Ads hidden for admins (apne ads par accidental click se bachao)
- [ ] Ads hidden for bots
- [ ] Minimum words for ads = 150 se zyada (thin pages par ads na chalein)
- [ ] Ad label neutral ho ("Advertisement") — "Click to support us" jaisa kuch nahi

### 3.4 Apply karne ka tareeqa

1. `adsense.google.com` par jayein aur apne Google account se sign in karein.
2. Website URL daalein: `https://pinecasttv.com`
3. Country aur payment details bharein (naam wahi ho jo bank account par hai).
4. AdSense aapko ek code dega. Use apne admin panel mein daalein:
   **Admin → Monetization → Settings** mein Publisher ID (`pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`) daalein aur AdSense **enable** karein. Code khud-ba-khud har page ke `<head>` mein lag jayega.
5. **Admin → Monetization → Ads.txt** kholein, suggested line save karein, phir `https://pinecasttv.com/ads.txt` check karein.
6. AdSense dashboard mein **"Request review"** dabayein.
7. **Intezaar karein.** Review mein aam tor par kuch din lagte hain, lekin **2 se 4 hafte** bhi lag sakte hain. Is dauran:
   - Site ko chalta rakhein, band na karein
   - Hafte mein 2–3 nayi posts publish karte rahein
   - Site ka design bar bar na badlein

### 3.5 Agar reject ho jaye

Ghabrayein nahi — pehli koshish mein reject hona bohat aam hai.

1. Rejection email mein di gayi wajah ghor se parhein.
2. **Kam az kam 2 hafte** intezaar karein, is dauran 10 nayi quality posts likhein.
3. Jo masla bataya gaya tha, us par khaas kaam karein.
4. Dobara apply karein. Baar baar (roz) apply karna nuqsan deta hai.

### 3.6 Approval ke baad

- **Admin → Monetization → Ad Units** mein har slot ka `data-ad-slot` ID daalein
- Sidebar ad mobile par **off** rakhein (readability)
- Video player ke bilkul paas ad na lagayein (accidental click policy)
- **Kabhi apne ads par click na karein** — aapke panel mein "hide ads for admins" isi liye on hai
- **Admin → Settings → Google Integrations** se AdSense API connect karein taake dashboard mein asli earnings dikhein
- Traffic kabhi kharidein nahi — invalid traffic par account foran band ho jata hai
